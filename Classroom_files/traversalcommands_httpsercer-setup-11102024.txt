Traversal commands and assertions 

========
Children
========
children
Get the children of each DOM element within a set of DOM elements.

The querying behavior of this command matches exactly how .children() works in jQuery.


syntax:

.children()
.children(selector)
.children(options)
.children(selector, options)

Usage:
cy.get('nav').children() // Yield children of nav

Yeilds:

Yields 
.children() yields the new DOM element(s) it found.
.children() is a query, and it is safe to chain further commands.


Children() chained with assertions 

cy.get('.left-nav>.nav').children().should('have.length', 8)
//it list down the all children belongs to .left-nav>.nav selector 

cy.get('.left-nav>.nav').children('.active').should('have.length', 1)
//list down the children with .active class .
